# Survivor inspection

Use a sequence of safe, information-gaining viewpoints. First seek a face,
torso contour, purposeful movement, or a reply; then seek an independent cue.
Do not convert a single silhouette score into a confirmed person. Preserve the
best frame, target crop, depth evidence, viewpoint ID, and observation time.
If the person may be conscious, stop at a safe distance, introduce Pulso, ask a
short yes/no question, and offer a simple nonverbal response such as two taps.
